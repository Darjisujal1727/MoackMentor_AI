AI INTERVIEW ENGINE

Meet Your AI Interviewer

MockMentor AI's AI Interviewer conducts structured technical mock interviews, asking questions tailored to your domain and difficulty. It reads each question aloud and listens to your response.

  • Domain-specific question banks (500+ questions)
  • Resume-aware personalised questions
  • Google TTS voice delivery for each question
  • Real-time transcription via Google STT

SMART ANALYTICS

Instant Feedback That Actually Helps

After every answer, MockMentor AI scores your response using a weighted algorithm that measures keyword coverage, response completeness, and technical depth.

  • Keyword-based scoring (60% weight)
  • Response length quality check (40% weight)
  • 1-to-5 star rating per question
  • Ideal answer shown for comparison
