FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

How does MockMentor AI interview work?
MockMentor AI generates tailored interview questions based on your skills, resume, and selected difficulty level. The AI Interviewer asks questions via text and voice. You can answer by typing or speaking, and receive instant scoring with feedback.
Is MockMentor AI free to use?
Yes! MockMentor AI is currently free to use. You can start a full mock interview session, receive real-time feedback, and download your performance report — all at no cost.
Which subjects and domains does MockMentor AI support?
We currently support JavaScript, Python, Java, React, SQL, HTML, CSS, and System Design. We are actively adding more domains including Data Structures, DevOps, and Cloud Computing.
How do I upload my resume for personalized questions?
On the Interview Setup page, you can upload your resume in PDF or DOCX format. Our system will automatically parse your education, skills, projects, and experience to generate personalized questions tailored to your background.
How long does a typical interview session last?
Each session has 10 questions with a 20-minute timer. You can answer by voice or text. The session typically takes 15–20 minutes, after which you can download a full PDF report with your scores and feedback.
Can I use MockMentor AI on mobile devices?
Yes, MockMentor AI is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. For the best voice recognition experience, we recommend using Google Chrome on Android or desktop.
How can I report a bug or suggest a new feature?
You can use the contact form on this page and select "Bug Report" or "Feature Request" as the topic. Our development team personally reviews all reports and suggestions.
