How It Works

From Zero to Interview-Ready
in 4 Simple Steps

MockMentor AI makes the entire interview preparation journey simple, guided, and effective. Here's exactly what happens when you use the platform.

QUICK OVERVIEW

The MockMentor AI Process

Four clear stages — each designed to maximise your preparation and growth.

01
Create Account

Sign up free using email, Google, or GitHub OAuth. Your profile is set up instantly.

02
Configure Interview

Choose your domain, upload resume, set difficulty. Everything is personalised to you.

03
Answer Questions

The AI Interviewer asks 10 questions by voice and text. Respond by typing or speaking.

04
Get Your Report

Receive instant scores per question and download a full PDF performance report.

STEP BY STEP

Every Step, Explained in Detail

Walk through each stage of the MockMentor AI experience — there are no surprises.

1
Step 1

Sign Up & Create Your Profile

Getting started takes under 60 seconds. Register with your email or sign in instantly via Google or GitHub OAuth — no long forms, no hassle.

  • Free registration — no credit card needed
  • One-click Google / GitHub login supported
  • Edit your name, email and profile anytime
  • Secure password hashing with Werkzeug
2
Step 2

Configure Your Interview Session

Before each session, you personalise it to match your exact preparation needs. Upload your resume and our AI parses it to generate questions based on what's actually on your profile.

  • Select subject domain (JS, Python, React, SQL…)
  • Choose difficulty: Easy, Medium, or Hard
  • Upload PDF or DOCX resume for personalisation
  • Configure number of questions (default: 10)
3
Step 3

The AI Interview Begins

Once the session starts, the AI Interviewer takes over. It reads each question aloud using text-to-speech and waits for your response. You can answer by typing or using your voice.

  • Google TTS reads each question out loud
  • Google STT transcribes your spoken answers
  • 20-minute countdown timer keeps you focused
  • Live performance metrics update after each answer
  • Skip or navigate between questions freely
4
Step 4

Submit & Receive Your Results

When you submit your session, our scoring engine evaluates every answer against the ideal answer using keyword matching and response quality analysis.

  • Per-question score (%) and star rating (1–5)
  • Ideal answer displayed for each question
  • Overall session score and performance grade
  • Download full PDF report for offline review
  • Session saved to your dashboard history

PRO TIPS

Get the Most From MockMentor AI

These tips will help you maximise the benefit of every single interview session you complete.

Use Voice for Realism

Answer by speaking rather than typing. It forces you to articulate your thoughts clearly, just like in a real interview. Chrome browser gives the best STT accuracy.

Upload a Detailed Resume

The more detail your resume contains (skills, projects, experience), the more personalised and relevant the questions will be to your background.

Start Easy, Then Increase Difficulty

Begin with Easy mode to build confidence, then progress to Medium and Hard. Gradual progression leads to the fastest improvement.

Review Ideal Answers After Every Session

The ideal answers in your report are crafted by domain experts. Study them after each session to learn the best way to structure your responses.

Track Progress Over Multiple Sessions

Your dashboard stores all previous sessions. Compare your scores over time to measure how much you're improving and where to focus next.
