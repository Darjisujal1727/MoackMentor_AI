OUR JOURNEY

The Story Behind MockMentor AI

From a college project to a platform helping thousands — here's how MockMentor AI came to be.

2023 — The Idea
Born in a College Dorm

A group of final-year engineering students struggled to find affordable mock interview tools. The frustration sparked the idea for MockMentor AI.

2024 — First Build
MVP Launched

The first version shipped with 10 question templates, voice recognition, and basic scoring. 500 students signed up in the first week.

2025 — Growth
AI Upgrade & Expansion

Integrated Google Speech-to-Text, resume parsing, PDF reports, difficulty levels, and expanded to 8 subject domains.

2026 — Today
10,000+ Students Empowered

MockMentor AI now serves thousands of students across colleges and universities, with a 4.8★ satisfaction rating and growing.
